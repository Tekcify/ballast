# Code of Conduct

Ballast follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and patient, assume good intent, and keep feedback about
the work rather than the person. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory
language are not welcome in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else
the project is represented.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainers privately through
the [Tekcify GitHub organisation](https://github.com/tekcify). Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or block
people who repeatedly break these standards, following the enforcement
guidelines in the Contributor Covenant.
